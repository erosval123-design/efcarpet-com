The quickest way to reach us

Email is quickest. Adding your order number to the subject helps us find it faster, and saying what you need in the first line helps us answer in one go. Phone lines are open during customer service hours; if nobody can pick up, a voicemail with your order number gets you a call back.

Write to us

Fill in this form and our reply will reach your inbox within 24 hours.

This opens your email app with the message addressed to contact@efcarpet.com. We use your details only to reply (see our Privacy Policy).

Questions we often answer

  • Will it fit? Send us the measurements of your space and the route from the road, and we will suggest what fits.
  • Car lift fit. Send your ceiling height, bay width, floor slab thickness and vehicle weight and we will check a lift against them.
  • Large-item delivery. Questions about kerbside delivery and booking a day: see also our Track Your Order page.
  • Changes and cancellations. Contact us as early as possible; before dispatch we can amend the order or cancel it in full.
  • Returns. Read our Returns & Refunds Policy and email us first: we send a return authorisation and the correct return address.

Response times

Most emails received during customer service hours get a same-day answer, and none waits longer than 24 hours. Messages sent overnight or on a bank holiday are answered on the next working day.

Before you chase a late delivery

Orders before 3pm (UK time) Monday to Friday are processed the same business day, dispatched within 1 to 2 business days and delivered 3 to 6 business days later (4 to 8 business days in total). Your dispatch email has a tracking link. If 4 to 8 business days have passed, email us with your order number and we will chase the carrier.
