Assembly

Fix the bracket into solid wall or studs, hang the fire and plug into a wall socket. About 45 minutes.

Care

Dust the glass with a soft cloth and never cover the heat outlet.

Before you order

Gas heaters are for outdoor use only, in open, well-ventilated areas, never inside a house, garage, tent or enclosed gazebo. Use a propane cylinder and regulator suited to the appliance and follow the clearance distances in the manual. Plug electric heaters straight into a wall socket, never into an extension lead or adaptor.

Questions

Can I use it without heat?

Yes. The flame effect can run on its own, using far less power.

Does it need a chimney?

No. It is electric, so there is no flue, gas or installation beyond the wall bracket.
